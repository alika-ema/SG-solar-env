Key Installation Requirements

1

Engage a Qualified Person (QP)

A registered Architect or Professional Engineer who ensures compliance with BCA, URA, and SCDF regulations.

2

Appoint a Licensed Electrical Worker (LEW)

Responsible for commissioning the system and applying for electrical licenses with SP Group.

3

SP Group Connection Application

Your LEW will apply for solar PV grid connection and handle metering requirements.

4

Generation License (if ≥1 MWac)

Systems with capacity of 1 MWac and above require a Generation License from EMA.
