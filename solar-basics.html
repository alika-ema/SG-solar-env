Solar Basics for Singapore Homes

How Solar Works

Solar photovoltaic (PV) systems convert sunlight directly into electricity using semiconductor materials. When sunlight hits the solar cells, it knocks electrons loose from their atoms, generating an electric current.


Key Components:

  • Solar Panels: Capture sunlight and convert it to DC electricity.
  • Inverter: Converts DC electricity to AC electricity for home use.
  • Meter: Measures energy generated and exported to the grid.
  • Mounting Structure: Secures panels to your roof.

Type Efficiency Lifespan Best For

Polycrystalline Medium (15-17%) 25 years Budget-conscious, larger roofs
